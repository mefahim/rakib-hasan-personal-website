Project 15 / Fashion Designer Portfolio

Tatiana Megard

Website design & development

Year
2023
Category
Fashion Designer Portfolio

Project overview

The Tatiana Megard website is an elegant and minimalist online portfolio. The website reflects her artistic philosophy, where fashion is celebrated as a craft, focusing on physical materiality and sharp, abstract geometric lines. Visitors can explore her unique design portfolio, gain insights into her creative process, and see her latest collections that redefine classic clothing concepts with an avant-garde approach.

Role on the project

As the web developer and designer for the Tatiana Megard website, I was responsible for creating a clean, visually appealing platform that aligns with her design ethos. My role included designing a sleek portfolio layout, integrating high-quality images of her collections, and ensuring seamless navigation. I also ensured the website is responsive and optimized for search engines to increase her visibility in the fashion industry.
