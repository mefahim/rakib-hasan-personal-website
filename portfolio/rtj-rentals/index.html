Project overview

RTJ Rentals provides exclusive corporate housing and short-term apartments for corporations, families, and individuals facing temporary displacement. With fully furnished, pet-friendly spaces in prime locations across Georgia and South Carolina, RTJ Rentals combines style, comfort, and functionality to create a home-away-from-home experience. Co-founded by Rodlyn and Tanya, the company focuses on personalized housing solutions…

Role on the project

As the developer for the RTJ Rentals website, I designed a platform that highlights the company’s premium temporary housing options. The site offers an easy-to-navigate interface for exploring property details, checking availability, and understanding the unique benefits of staying with RTJ Rentals. My goal was to create a seamless user experience that reflects the company’s values of comfort and care.
