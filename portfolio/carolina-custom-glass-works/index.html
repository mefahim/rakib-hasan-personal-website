Project overview

Carolina Custom Glass Works is a specialized website designed to showcase premium glass solutions for both residential and commercial spaces. The website emphasizes custom glass craftsmanship, offering a variety of products such as custom shower enclosures, storefronts, windows, glass railings, and closet systems, tailored to enhance the aesthetic appeal and functionality of any space.

Role on the project

As the web developer and designer for Carolina Custom Glass Works, I was responsible for building a visually appealing and functional website that showcases their custom glass solutions. My role included designing a clean, user-friendly interface, developing detailed service pages, integrating a portfolio section, and ensuring the site is responsive and optimized for SEO to attract and engage potential clients.
