Project overview

La Côte De Beaune offers a premier delicatessen and catering counter service designed to delight both the eyes and the palate. Established by Michel and Thomas in 1994, the establishment blends their extensive experience with renowned Belgian and French cuisine to deliver a unique culinary experience.

Role on the project

As the web developer and designer for La Côte De Beaune, I was responsible for crafting a sophisticated and user-friendly website that reflects the elegance and quality of the delicatessen and catering services. My role involved designing a visually appealing interface that highlights the gourmet nature of the offerings, integrates detailed information about the delicatessen and catering services, and features high-quality images of the dishes.
