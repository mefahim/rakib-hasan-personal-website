Project overview

RedKey Logistics LLC is a premier transportation provider offering comprehensive logistics solutions, including flatbeds, step decks, hot shots, dedicated box trucks, and specialized heavy haul services. With guaranteed capacity at major U.S. ports and access to over 50,000 vetted carriers, RedKey Logistics excels in providing timely and reliable freight solutions. The company specializes in truckload, less-than-truckload (LTL)…

Role on the project

For the RedKey Logistics website, I developed a streamlined platform that highlights their extensive transportation services and commitment to exceptional service. My role involved designing an intuitive site that showcases their capabilities in freight hauling and logistics, emphasizes their rapid response times, and provides easy access to information on their diverse shipping solutions. The site effectively communicates RedKey Logistics’ dedication to quality and efficiency in the logistics industry.
