Project overview

Stericlean is dedicated to providing top-quality cleaning and disinfection services specifically tailored for dental and medical practices. The company focuses on creating a clean and healthy environment to ensure the highest standards of hygiene and safety for healthcare establishments.

Role on the project

As the web developer for Stericlean, I was responsible for designing and developing a website that effectively showcases the company’s specialized cleaning and disinfection services. The goal of the website is to provide a seamless experience for visitors seeking reliable cleaning and disinfection services, and to establish Stericlean as a leading provider in the healthcare cleaning sector.
